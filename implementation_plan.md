# 🚀 Implementation Plan: Karmaa Productions Web Application

Design and develop a world-class, ultra-luxurious, audio-visual web application for **Karmaa Productions**—a bespoke music composition, sound design, and cinematic film production house.

---

## 🎯 Architectural & Mind-Blowing Visual Design Principles

1. **State-of-the-Art Luxury Aesthetics (No Emojis, Ultra-Professional):**
   * **Obsidian & Champagne Gold Color Palette:** Deep space obsidian `#07080b`, luminous gold `#e2b45e`, glowing metallic amber `#ffaa00`, and frosted glassmorphism overlays.
   * **Zero Emojis Policy:** Exclusively use bespoke SVG line-art icons (music notes, cinema reels, soundwave spectrums, vinyl discs) for a high-end corporate luxury finish.
   * **Typography:** `Cinzel` / `Playfair Display` for regal cinematic headlines paired with `Outfit` for sleek modern UI text.
   * **Professional Infinite Marquees (Ticker Banners):** Smooth hardware-accelerated CSS marquee banners displaying brand pillars, services, and music genres in luxury typography.
2. **Interactive & Entertaining Audio-Visual Features:**
   * **Cinematic Video & Audio Showcase Modal:** Embedded video player overlay for watching 4K sample music videos, studio BTS clips, and film scoring trailers.
   * **Web Audio API Realtime Visualizer:** Live frequency spectrum canvas and reactive soundwave rings that pulse dynamically when audio samples play.
   * **Interactive "Sound & Vibe Studio" (Intake Quiz):** Interactive multi-step studio mixer interface where visitors pick music moods (Sufi, Orchestral, Acoustic, Synth) and preview audio visualizers before submitting custom inquiries.
   * **Interactive 3D Glass Tilt Cards:** Dynamic cursor-following light reflections on portfolio items.
3. **SEO Optimization Engine:**
   * Semantic HTML5 (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<article>`, `<footer>`).
   * Rich Open Graph & Twitter Card meta tags.
   * **JSON-LD Schema Markup:** `MusicGroup`, `AudioObject`, `VideoObject`, and `ProfessionalService` structured data for Google Search rich snippets.
   * SEO-optimized Heading Hierarchy (`<h1>` per section) and unique element IDs.

## 📑 Proposed Web Application Components

### Component 1: Meta & SEO Head Structure
* **File:** `index.html`
* **SEO Inclusions:**
  * Title: `Karmaa Productions | Bespoke Music, BGM Scoring & Cinematic Films`
  * Meta Description: `India's premier bespoke music composition & film studio. Custom love anthems, cinematic pre-wedding videos, feature film BGM scores, and commercial sonic branding.`

### Component 2: Core Design Tokens & Dynamic Stylesheet
* **File:** `index.css`
* Design system definitions: HSL color palettes, custom audio waveform animations, glow effects, responsive typography (Inter & Playfair Display / Outfit fonts), glassmorphism utility classes.

### Component 3: Navigation Bar & Persistent Floating Audio Player
* **Header & Floating Player:** Sleek glass header with links (Love Anthems, Film Scoring, Commercial Sound, Our Process, Inquire).
* **Persistent Music Controller:** Play/Pause, Track Selector, Animated Equalizer Bars, Volume Slider.

### Component 4: Hero Section (Audio-Visual Immersion)
* Headline: *"Don't Just Record Moments. Give Them An Original Soundtrack."*
* Dual CTAs: `Listen to Anthems` & `Request Bespoke Proposal`.
* Background: Ambient cinematic video / glowing soundwave canvas.

### Component 5: Dual Portfolio Showcase (B2C & B2B Tabs)
* **Tab 1: Personal & Celebrations (B2C)**

### Component 6: The 4-Step Karmaa Journey (Process Section)
* Step 1: Discovery & Story Interview
* Step 2: Custom Song & BGM Composition
