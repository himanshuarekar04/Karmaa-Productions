## 💰 4. BUSINESS MODEL & BESPOKE INQUIRY STRUCTURE

> **Pricing Policy:**  
> *Karmaa Productions operates on an exclusive, High-Touch Bespoke Consultation Model. Public pricing is not disclosed. Every project—from personal love anthems to feature film background scoring—is custom-quoted based on project scope, instrumentation, scale of shoot, location, and commercial licensing rights.*

### 🎯 Inquiry-Based Service Framework

1. **B2C Personal & Storytelling Projects:**  
   * **Custom Quote Triggers:** Length of track, vocal arrangement (solo vs duet vs choir), location of shoot, video production scale (1-day vs multi-day destination shoot), Spotify publishing, physical Vinyl keepsakes.  
   * **Call To Action:** *"Request a Private Consultation & Story Brief"*

2. **B2B Media, Film & Commercial Commissions:**  
   * **Custom Quote Triggers:** Commercial broadcast rights (Local / National / Global), campaign duration, orchestral vs electronic score complexity, audio stems delivery requirements.  
   * **Call To Action:** *"Inquire for Commercial / Film Scoring Brief"*
